# Third-party instrument samples

The built-in sampled instruments are distributed under their own licenses. The music2-gen source code remains MIT licensed.

## Grand piano

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/ . The `grand-piano` instrument uses a trimmed selection of the original samples, with an SFZ mapping prepared for music2-gen. Attribution must be retained when redistributing these samples.

## String ensemble

VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios. Licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE . The `strings` and `strings-staccato` instruments use selections of these samples with SFZ mappings prepared for music2-gen.
